About

I am a photographer working from a small studio in Leeds. Most of my pictures are of places in the hour after everyone has left.

I work slowly, usually with one camera and one lens, and print everything myself in the back room. A picture is finished when I can look at it for a week without wanting to change it.

In recent years my work has been shown in small galleries and libraries around the region and sold as prints to people who wanted something quiet for a wall.
