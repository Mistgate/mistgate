About us

Seam & Thread is a workshop of two on the first floor of a building in Norwich, above a shop that sells wool.

We learnt the trade from people who had done it for forty years, and we have kept some of their habits, such as always pinning twice.

If you are not sure whether something is worth altering, bring it in. We will tell you honestly.
