About this page

01

What this page shows

This page shows the live state of the Brightstack services and a record of past incidents. It is kept up to date by the team that runs them.

02

How uptime is measured

Every service is checked from several places once a minute. A minute counts as down when more than half of the checks fail.

03

How we report incidents

We post an update when we start looking into a problem, when we find the cause and when it is resolved. Larger incidents get a short write-up afterwards.

04

Planned maintenance

Planned work is announced here at least three days ahead and takes place at quiet hours.
