Trouble signing in?

Most sign-in problems have a simple cause. Work through these steps before you contact anyone.

Still stuck?

Ask the person who manages Oakrise for your organisation to reset your password. For security reasons we cannot reset it over the phone or by email.

  1. Check that Caps Lock is off; passwords are case sensitive.
  2. Look for a stray space at the start or end of your username.
  3. Try again in a new browser window with no saved form data.
  4. Wait ten minutes if you have tried several times without success.

Privacy notice

We keep as little about you as we can and look after what we keep. This is a short account of what that is and why.

What we keep

Your username, the times you signed in and the details that belong to your account. Nothing else.

What we use it for

To let you sign in and to protect your account from anyone else. We do not sell information about our users.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the service needs in order to work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Ask the person who manages your account.
