About

I am Amelia. I read in the evenings and on the train, and I write a few lines about each book so that I remember it.

These are not reviews. I rarely say whether a book is good. I say what I noticed, what I underlined, and what I kept thinking about on the walk to work.

Currently

  • Rereading a book from school
  • Working through a very long novel in small doses
  • Returning three overdue library books
