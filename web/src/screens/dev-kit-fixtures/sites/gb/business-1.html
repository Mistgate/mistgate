About us

Hanger Workshop is a small workshop in Exeter that has been cutting mitres since long before anyone in the building was born.

We frame a lot of children's drawings and a few very old maps. Both get the same attention and the same acid-free card.

Bring us something you care about and we will find the frame it has been waiting for.
