About

I draw for a living, mostly for picture books and the occasional poster. I work from a small room in Nottingham with a window onto a car park, which I draw as if it were a lake.

Before illustration I studied textile printing, and the habit of thinking in layers never quite left. Most of my pictures are built from three or four flat colours.
