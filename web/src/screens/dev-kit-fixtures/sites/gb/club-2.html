Join us

How to join

We are a friendly group of about sixty walkers, from people who have walked all their lives to people who started last month.

You do not need special kit. Boots, a waterproof and something to drink are plenty. If in doubt, come and ask on Sunday morning.

  1. Come along to any meeting and say hello.
  2. Try it for a month before you decide anything.
  3. Pay the membership fee to the treasurer when you are ready.

Questions

How fast do you walk?

At the pace of the slowest person on the day, which is often the leader.

Can I bring my dog?

On most walks, yes, on a lead. The programme says which walks are not suitable.

What if it rains?

We walk anyway. The pub stop is longer.
