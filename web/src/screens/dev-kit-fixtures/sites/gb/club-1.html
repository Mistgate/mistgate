Looking up, together

We meet once a month to hear a talk and, when the sky allows, to look through telescopes. You do not need to own a telescope, or even know where north is.

How to join

We are about thirty members, with telescopes ranging from a pair of binoculars to something the size of a washing machine.

Come to any meeting. If the sky is clear we will show you the Moon, Jupiter or whatever else is up.

  1. Come along to any meeting and say hello.
  2. Try it for a month before you decide anything.
  3. Pay the membership fee to the treasurer when you are ready.

Questions

Do I need a telescope?

No. Members are happy to share theirs, and the society owns one you can borrow.

What should I wear for an observing night?

More than you think. Hat, gloves and a flask.

What if it is cloudy?

We have a talk indoors, and sometimes the cloud goes away at ten o'clock.
