About

I am Amelia. I cook for a family of four in a kitchen in Norwich that is smaller than most people's bathrooms, and I would not change it.

I write down what worked, in the form I actually cook it, which is to say with fewer amounts and more adjectives than a proper recipe.

Currently

  • Feeding a new sourdough starter
  • Salting lemons for spring
  • Making peace with the oven door
